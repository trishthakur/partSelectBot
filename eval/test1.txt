### Understanding the Problem: Why Is My Refrigerator Too Cold?

When a refrigerator is excessively cold, it often leads to frozen food in the fresh food compartment. The most common culprits are faulty temperature control components. Let's walk through the diagnostic steps.

---

### Step-by-Step Troubleshooting Guide

#### **1. Check the Thermostat / Temperature Control**

This is the most likely cause. The thermostat tells the compressor when to turn on and off to maintain the set temperature. If it's stuck in the "on" position, the compressor will run continuously, making the fridge too cold.

*   **What to do:** Turn the thermostat knob from a colder setting to a warmer setting and listen for a faint "click." If you don't hear a click, it could be a sign the thermostat is faulty and needs replacement.

#### **2. Inspect the Damper Door Control**

Many modern refrigerators have a damper door that opens and closes to let cold air from the freezer into the refrigerator compartment. If this door gets stuck open, too much cold air will flow in, freezing your fresh food.

*   **What to do:** Locate the damper (usually at the top of the refrigerator section or in the back wall). See if it's open when the refrigerator is at its set temperature. If it seems stuck, the damper control assembly may need to be replaced.

#### **3. Test the Thermistor**

The thermistor is a sensor that monitors the temperature and sends the reading to the main control board. If it gives an incorrect (too warm) reading, the control board will keep the compressor running to cool down a fridge that is already cold enough.

*   **What to do:** Thermistors can be tested for resistance with a multimeter, but this requires some technical skill. If the thermostat seems fine, the thermistor is the next component to suspect.

#### **4. Consider the Main Control Board (Less Common)**

If the thermostat, damper, and thermistor all check out, the issue could be with the main control board itself. It might be failing to process the signals correctly. This is a more complex and expensive part to replace.

---

### Common Parts You Might Need

While I don't have a specific list of parts for your exact model, the parts most commonly associated with a "too cold" refrigerator are:

*   **Temperature Control Thermostat**
